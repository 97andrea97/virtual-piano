How to play

Rest your fingers over the keys, then press with a quick, short downward tap of one finger that stops, like on a real piano. The key that plays is the one lit up under that finger before it moved. Lift the finger to release the note. Moving the whole hand, sliding across keys, or fingers that just get dragged along by another finger don't play.

Air Piano

A piano drawn on your camera image. Your fingers are tracked by the camera — press the virtual keys and they play.

  1. Place your phone, tablet or laptop so the camera sees your hands.
  2. Rest your hands so your fingertips are over the keys of the piano drawn on the picture, as if it were lying on a table in front of you.
  3. Play a key with a quick, short downward tap of one finger. Just hovering over a key never plays it.
  4. Use Adjust to move, resize or tilt the keyboard.

Video is processed on your device and never uploaded. You can also tap the keys on screen.
